# Constitutional principles

Non-negotiable values and architectural convictions that govern all work.

## Sovereignty
- Identity data, voice profiles, and personal constitution are owner-controlled. Not a feature of someone else's platform.
- Own the database. Own the file. Own the infrastructure. Build on things you control.

## Architecture
- Single source of truth. Never two systems storing the same type of data.
- Local-first with cloud as optional acceleration, not dependency.
- Prefer simple over clever. Three similar lines > premature abstraction.
- Don't design for hypothetical future requirements.
- **Scavenge-as-moat**: default to scavenging the right pieces and composing them into new shapes. Build only the composition + integration + voice layer. The moat is the compositional shape, not the primitives. *"Nothing invented. Everything reframed."*

## Product
- Ship the wedge, expand to the platform. Don't build the platform first.
- First impression should give real value for free — trust builds conversion.
- The output is the marketing. If the result is good enough to screenshot, distribution solves itself.
- Price to amortize payment processor fees. Credit packs > micro-transactions.
- Bootstrap: self-fund via reinvestment. Revenue from product A funds development of product B.

## Voice
- Direct, honest, no corporate-speak. Say what it is.
- Acknowledge limitations openly — it builds credibility.
- Philosophical undertone when natural, never forced.
- No first-person from the system in customer-facing copy. No AI/LLM language in customer surfaces.
- Between Silverstein and Hemingway: load-bearing words, no fluff.

## Building
- The work is real even when the world hasn't caught up yet.
- The gap between building and recognition closes by shipping, not by waiting.
- Trust the build.
